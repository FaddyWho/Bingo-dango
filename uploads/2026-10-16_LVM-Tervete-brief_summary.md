# Client brief: LVM Nature Park in Tērvete (2026-10-16)

> **Source type: client context.** This is not participant evidence (P, FT, WS) and not team work (TW).
> All figures are as reported by LVM. We have not checked them.
> **The client's challenges are framing for us to investigate, not a scope we have to deliver.** Section 6 restates them as open questions.

## 1. Client
- JSC "Latvia's State Forests" (LVM). Founded 1999, about 1,200 employees. It manages state forests for both conservation and income.
- The brief comes from the **Recreational and environmental division**. Its main sites are:
  - Spāre
  - Jaunmoku Palace and Forest Museum
  - Vijciems Pine Cone Drying Kiln
  - Ezernieki
  - Kalsnava Arboretum
  - **Nature Park in Tērvete and Nature Park Glamping**
- **Focus site: LVM Nature Park in Tērvete.** WS01 took place in Kuldīga with Kuldīga locals. That is the workshop location, not the park.

## 7. Overlaps with WS01 to check (not conclusions)
- **Group C name:** the adult group's name, "Sprīdītis", is a Brigadere character in the park's Fairy Tale Forest. Did the facilitators assign the name or did the group choose it?
- **Adult price:** WS01 adults priced entry at 20 EUR (facilitator note, B01), the same as the park's family ticket. This may be prior knowledge from the LVM staff in the group.
- **Free access (T03):** the park already has a free zone (Kurbad's Land) and free entry for children under 7.
- **Teens as a segment:** teens 16–18 are not a listed visitor group (students are 6%). Why we focus on teens should be stated in `01_plan.md`.
- **Distance:** WS01 asked how far the park should be from Kuldīga. The real distance from Kuldīga to Tērvete is not in the files.
