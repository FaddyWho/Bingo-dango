## 2. Conventions (apply to every board)

1. **Every sticky carries a source tag:** session ID plus segment, for example `WS01-T` (teen) or `WS01-A` (adult). Interviews use `P01`, field trips `FT01`. Client material uses `CB01`.
2. **Colour code on WS01 stickies:** yellow = Group A, blue = Group B (teen groups), red = Group C (adults). Teens and adults never share a lane. Never merge segments without saying so.
3. **Segment mapping is read from facilitator notes on B01** (the note on free money says "both teenager groups" versus "the grownups/LVM people"). Confirm with Anna against `02_participants.md`.
4. **Anonymous only.** Participant IDs, no names, schools, contact details or identifiable photos. Participants under 18 stay anonymous.
5. **Check sticky authorship.** FigJam shows who wrote each sticky. Copies made on 2026-11-12 show the original author label. Before sharing a board outside the team, check what shows and that no participant names appear.
6. **Adults in WS01 are park professionals (LVM) plus a teacher**, not typical visitors. Say so on any board that shows adult views.
7. **Quote exactly or don't quote.** Stickies that quote a participant must match the transcript or summary wording.
8. **Traceability.** Each cluster names its theme (T), and insights (I) where they apply, from `03_synthesis.md`.
9. **Do not delete.** Move old board versions to `_archive/` as a link or export, with the date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) in the name.
10. **Client framing is not evidence.** Client challenges (CF01–CF07) can sit on the board as questions, in their own lane, tagged `CB01`.
