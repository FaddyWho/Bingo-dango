# 01 Plan
## Objective
(draft) Understand why people visit, return to, or skip LVM Nature Park in Tērvete, and where the visit loses excitement, to inform the park's value proposition and visitor journey.

## Client brief
- Source: `uploads/2026-10-16_LVM-Tervete-brief_summary.md` (CB01, received 2026-10-16).
- The client's challenges (CF01–CF07: value proposition, family marketing, retention, new gateways, storytelling, wayfinding, pacing) are **starting hypotheses, not our scope**. Research decides which problems we work on (D004, proposed).
- What the client cares about most, in their words (paraphrased): a value proposition that works for both Latvian and foreign visitors, and overcoming locals' "been there, done that" fatigue.
## Research questions
Draft, to confirm with the team:
- **RQ1:** What gives locals and visitors a reason to go to, or return to, a nature park like Tērvete? (CF01, CF03)
- **RQ2:** How do different groups (teens, families, foreign visitors) relate to folklore and nature in the park? (CF01, CF05)
- **RQ3:** Where in the visit journey do people lose excitement, energy or direction? (CF06, CF07)
