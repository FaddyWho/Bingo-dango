# 03 Synthesis
Evidence so far: WS01 only (teens T = 12, adults A = 4). Confidence stays low until FT01 and interviews are added.
The client brief (CB01) is context only, not evidence. See "Link to client framing" below.

## Themes
### T01: Activities and experiences give a reason to visit
- **Description:** Parks are imagined as places to do something. Skiing, skating, climbing, volleyball, cat petting and more.
- **Evidence:** WS01 (T and A)
- **Counter-evidence:** none yet
- **Confidence:** medium

### T02: The park is a social experience
- **Description:** Visits are imagined with friends or family, plus ways to document them (photo booths).
- **Evidence:** WS01 (mostly T)
- **Confidence:** low-medium

### T03: Free access matters
- **Description:** Teens strongly want free entry. Some imagined paying visitors. 2 of 3 groups mentioned free.
- **Evidence:** WS01 (T strongly)
- **Context (CB01, not evidence):** Tērvete already has a free-access zone (Kurbad's Land) and free entry for children under 7. Adult ticket 7.00 EUR, family ticket 20.00 EUR.
- **Confidence:** medium

### T04: Animals everywhere
- **Description:** Every park concept included animal features.
- **Evidence:** WS01 (3/3 groups)
- **Confidence:** medium

### T05: Nature as decoration vs nature as setting
- **Description:** Teen ideas treated nature as decoration. Adults treated it as the place where activities happen. In the building task, nature became a focal point linking experiences (waterfalls, old trees) for both.
- **Evidence:** WS01 Activities 4 and 5 (T vs A)
- **Confidence:** low-medium

### T06: Adventure and nature park blur, folklore is classified differently
- **Description:** No clear difference between adventure and nature park. Teens place folklore in adventure parks, adults in nature parks.
- **Evidence:** WS01 Activity 2 (T vs A)
- **Context (CB01, not evidence):** Tērvete's signature zones are folklore-based (Brigadere fairy tales, gnomes). The client places the park as attraction-based.
- **Confidence:** low-medium

### T07: Connected flow and seasonal change
- **Description:** Attractions are linked into circular flows. Seasonal and festive activities and mood changes appear.
- **Evidence:** WS01 Activity 5
- **Confidence:** low

## Insights
| ID | Insight | Themes | Evidence | Implication |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I01 | Visitors need a clear reason to go, and the park currently lacks excitement | T01, T02 | WS01 | Explore magic and adventure, and map the in-park journey for excitement gaps |
| I02 | "Free" signals value, so a paid ticket may need something given in return | T03 | WS01 Activity 6 | Test a free drink, seasonal gift, or tiered pricing |
| I03 | Nature works best when it is the connector between experiences | T05, T07 | WS01 Activities 4-5 | Design activities around natural focal points (water, old trees) |

## Opportunity areas
- Folklore and fairy-tale experiences that still feel like a nature park
- Tiered pricing: low entry plus paid experiences
- Seasonal and winter programming
- Photo spots and shared experiences for peer groups and families

## Open questions
- Where else do locals go for excitement?
- How do visitor needs differ between peer groups and families?
- Do adults who are not park professionals share the teen view on price?
- Is "been there, done that" fatigue real, and for whom? (CF01)
- Do foreign visitors need folklore explained, or is the barrier something else? (CF01)
